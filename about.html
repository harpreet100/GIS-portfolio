About Me

GIS with a Technical Foundation

Combining geographic thinking, municipal GIS experience, computer science, spatial analysis, cartography, and automation.

Background

I am completing a Bachelor of Geographic Information Systems at the Southern Alberta Institute of Technology. I also have a diploma in Computer Science from Kwantlen Polytechnic University.

This combination allows me to approach GIS work through spatial analysis and cartographic communication while also understanding programming, databases, structured problem-solving, data processing, and workflow automation.

GIS Practicum Experience

I completed a GIS practicum at Rocky View County, where I worked alongside experienced GIS professionals in a municipal and county environment.

My practicum experience included planning map sets, spatial and attribute queries, custom-radius property information, custom map production, public GIS inquiries, ArcGIS web and field applications, Python automation, FME workflows, and collaboration with different County departments.

The experience taught me how GIS supports planning, operations, public service, cross-department collaboration, and evidence-based decision-making.

Internal County information, restricted property data, private workflows, and confidential organizational deliverables are not published in this portfolio.

Professional Approach

I learned that successful GIS work requires more than knowing how to use GIS software. Professional GIS also depends on accurate data, clear requirements, organized workflows, appropriate documentation, quality control, communication, and understanding the purpose of the final product.

When responding to a GIS request, I try to understand the operational question first. I then identify the relevant data, determine the appropriate GIS method, and select a map, analysis, dataset, or application format that meets the user's needs.

Career Direction

I am pursuing GIS analyst opportunities with a long-term interest in GIS development and automation. I am especially interested in municipal, government, environmental, transportation, planning, and utility organizations.

My goal is to continue developing skills in spatial analysis, data management, Python and ArcPy automation, FME, web GIS, cartography, and geospatial application development.
