About Me

Bridging the gap between Spatial Analysis and Software Development.

Education & Background

I am currently completing my Bachelor of Geographic Information Systems (BGIS) at the Southern Alberta Institute of Technology (SAIT). Prior to focusing on geospatial technology, I earned a diploma in Computer Science from Kwantlen Polytechnic University.

This dual background allows me to approach GIS not just as an analyst using out-of-the-box tools, but as a developer who can construct scripts, manage databases, automate workflows, and integrate APIs.

Practical Experience

I recently completed a GIS practicum in a county/municipal environment. This role provided hands-on exposure to real-world GIS operations, including spatial data management, ArcGIS Pro mapping, workflow documentation, and practical data processes within a local government setting.

Career Direction

I am pursuing roles as a GIS Analyst with a direct trajectory toward GIS Developer and Automation Specialist positions. I am particularly interested in opportunities within municipal, government, environmental, transportation, or utility organizations where spatial data combined with programmed automation can solve complex operational problems.
