Practicum Overview

I completed my GIS practicum with Rocky View County, where I worked alongside experienced GIS professionals in a municipal and county environment.

The practicum allowed me to apply concepts from my Bachelor of Geographic Information Systems studies to real operational, planning, public-service, and organizational requirements.

My work involved spatial-data management, cartographic production, planning support, public GIS inquiries, web GIS, field applications, and workflow automation. I also communicated with staff from different County departments to understand their needs and develop practical GIS products and solutions.

Experience summary: Supported municipal GIS operations by producing planning map sets, running spatial and attribute queries, responding to custom mapping requests, working with field and web GIS applications, and developing Python and FME automation workflows.

Planning and Decision Support

I prepared maps and spatial information to support planning processes and organizational decision-making. This included querying County data, reviewing geographic and tabular information, and producing map sets that communicated development activity and changes occurring within Rocky View County.

  • Created circulation map sets to communicate proposed developments and planning-related changes.
  • Ran spatial and attribute queries to identify relevant geographic and property information.
  • Prepared maps and supporting spatial information for staff and Council decision-making.
  • Produced custom-radius property ownership information for the Planning Department using County GIS and assessment data.
  • Selected appropriate geographic features, map extents, labels, symbology, and supporting information for each request.
  • Created clear cartographic products for planning, internal review, and public communication.

Custom Mapping and Public GIS Support

I gained direct experience responding to GIS inquiries from County staff and members of the public. This work required understanding each request, identifying the appropriate datasets, and presenting geographic information in a useful and understandable format.

  • Responded to internal and walk-in GIS inquiries.
  • Discussed mapping needs with the person requesting the information.
  • Identified the appropriate datasets, scale, map extent, labels, and supporting information for each request.
  • Created custom maps for planning, property, operational, and general information requirements.
  • Reviewed available GIS data before producing the requested map or spatial information.
  • Communicated GIS information to both technical and non-technical users.

Spatial Data Management and Analysis

I worked with both spatial and tabular datasets and gained practical experience understanding how municipal GIS data is maintained, analyzed, queried, and used by different departments.

  • Worked with municipal spatial and attribute datasets.
  • Used ArcGIS Pro for data review, spatial analysis, geoprocessing, and map production.
  • Ran queries to identify features and records matching specific operational or planning requirements.
  • Reviewed data for accuracy, consistency, completeness, and suitability.
  • Applied data-management and file-organization practices used in a professional GIS environment.
  • Learned how dependable GIS data supports planning, operations, public service, and organizational decision-making.

Cartography and Map Production

Cartographic production was an important part of my practicum. I created maps for different purposes and audiences while considering clarity, visual hierarchy, scale, labels, symbology, and the intended use of each product.

  • Created professional map layouts using ArcGIS Pro.
  • Selected appropriate symbols and colours for different geographic features.
  • Applied clear labelling and visual hierarchy.
  • Adjusted scale and extent based on the intended purpose of the map.
  • Produced both standardized map sets and custom mapping products.
  • Considered the knowledge and needs of the intended audience when presenting spatial information.

Web GIS and Field Applications

I worked with Esri web and field technologies used to make geographic information available outside the traditional desktop GIS environment.

  • Worked with ArcGIS Field Maps to support field access to geographic information.
  • Gained experience with ArcGIS Experience Builder.
  • Worked with ArcGIS Online content and web-mapping applications.
  • Explored custom GIS websites designed for internal or public use.
  • Learned how web applications can provide task-specific spatial information to different County departments.
  • Developed a stronger understanding of how desktop GIS, hosted data, web maps, applications, and field workflows connect.

Python and FME Automation

I developed and explored GIS automation workflows using Python, FME, and FME Flow. These workflows demonstrated how repetitive data-processing tasks can be standardized, scheduled, and completed more consistently.

  • Developed and explored Python-based GIS automation workflows.
  • Used Python and GIS scripting concepts to reduce repetitive processing steps.
  • Used FME to transform, process, validate, and manage spatial and tabular datasets.
  • Worked with FME Flow concepts for internal and automated data-processing workflows.
  • Considered consistency, repeatability, data validation, troubleshooting, and error handling.
  • Learned how automation can reduce manual effort while improving the reliability of routine GIS processes.

Cross-Department Collaboration

An important part of my practicum involved communicating with different County departments. This helped me understand that a successful GIS solution begins with understanding the operational question, not simply choosing a GIS tool.

  • Communicated with staff from multiple departments to understand their GIS and data requirements.
  • Asked questions to clarify the intended purpose of a map, analysis, dataset, or application.
  • Translated operational questions into practical GIS tasks.
  • Identified appropriate data, analytical methods, and presentation formats.
  • Developed GIS products based on the needs of the requesting department.
  • Learned how GIS supports planning, operations, public service, and evidence-based decision-making.

What I Learned

The practicum showed me how GIS functions in a professional municipal environment. It helped me understand how spatial information supports planning, operations, organizational decision-making, public service, and collaboration between departments.

I learned that successful GIS work requires more than technical software knowledge. It also requires accurate data, organized workflows, appropriate documentation, quality control, clear communication, and an understanding of the decision that the final product is intended to support.

Working directly with experienced GIS professionals strengthened my troubleshooting skills and exposed me to professional GIS standards, workflow design, operational requirements, and best practices.

The experience also showed me how my computer science background can complement GIS through scripting, automation, data processing, application development, and structured problem-solving.
