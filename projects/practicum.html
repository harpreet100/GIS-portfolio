1. Operational Problem & Scope

Embedded within Rocky View County's GIS team, I supported day-to-day municipal spatial requests spanning Planning, Operations, and public walk-in inquiries. Each request required translating non-technical operational questions into verified datasets, appropriate map scales, and clear cartographic or web-based deliverables.

Confidentiality & Data Governance: Internal Rocky View County datasets, property ownership records, internal URLs, restricted development files, and private automation scripts are excluded from this public portfolio.

2. Data & Spatial Pipeline

Planning & Council Decision Support

  • Produced standardized circulation map sets illustrating proposed developments and land-use changes for staff and Council review.
  • Executed spatial and attribute queries to generate custom-radius property ownership notification lists using County GIS and assessment datasets.
  • Verified data completeness, attribute consistency, and projection alignment prior to cartographic export.

Custom Mapping & Public GIS Support

  • Responded to internal departmental requests and walk-in public GIS inquiries by scoping the exact geographic extent, parcel context, and required layers.
  • Applied cartographic visual hierarchy, clear labeling, and audience-specific symbology in ArcGIS Pro.

3. Web-GIS, Field Apps & Automation Architecture

  • Web & Field GIS: Worked with ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS Experience Builder, and ArcGIS Field Maps to connect hosted municipal layers to browser and mobile field workflows.
  • Python & FME Automation: Developed and explored Python scripting and FME / FME Flow workspaces to standardize repetitive spatial transformations, schema validation, and scheduled processing with error handling.
